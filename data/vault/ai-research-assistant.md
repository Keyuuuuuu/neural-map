# AI Research Assistant

An intelligent workflow agent designed to monitor scientific preprints (arXiv/bioRxiv), parse research papers, extract methodologies, and dynamically link them into a personal knowledge vault.

## Capabilities

- **Automatic Citation Parsing**: Crawls bibliographical structures and resolves DOI metadata.
- **Methodology Cross-Referencing**: Employs sentence transformers to map related theorems and algorithms.
- **Conversational Synthesis**: Incorporates LLMs to answer domain-specific research questions.
